2. What personal data we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to give us directly, through the forms on this website:

  • Contact form: name, email address, and the message you submit.
  • Newsletter form: email address.

We do not use tracking scripts, analytics, or advertising cookies, so we do not collect any data passively as you browse the site.

3. How we use your data

Data submitted through the Contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. Data submitted through the Newsletter form is used solely to send you updates from Valsamis, until you unsubscribe. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process Contact form data based on our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries you have initiated. We process Newsletter form data based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

5. How long we keep your data

Contact form submissions are kept only as long as necessary to handle and resolve your inquiry. Newsletter subscriber data is kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated and, where required, a consent banner will be added before any such cookies are set.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to our processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for newsletter subscriptions)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (ΑΠΔΠΧ / HDPA), www.dpa.gr

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
